# The World Organization of Women Nations Womafreesm Inc.

**Womafreesm: a global infrastructure for women's influence.**

Womafreesm is a global infrastructure for women's influence and an international nonprofit organization. It transforms women's lived experiences into data and evidence that can become the basis for new rules, laws, standards, and decisions. Founded in 2025 by Marina Sukhomlinova, Founder & President, Womafreesm is registered in the United States and recognized as a 501(c)(3) Public Charity.
